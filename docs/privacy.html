Privacy policy

Viral Shorts Pipeline. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Viral Shorts Pipeline ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner on the owner's own computer. It has no other users and no servers of its own.

Google data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel.
  • The channel's own recent uploads and their public statistics (such as view counts).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload Shorts to the owner's channel, to avoid posting the same Short twice, and to rank future topics by how past Shorts performed. It is not used for advertising and is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Where the data is stored

The sign-in token and all results are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google's own YouTube API. The owner can delete everything at any time by deleting the app's data folder, and can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google policies

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be raised on the app's GitHub page.
